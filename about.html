About

Brendan Kesterson is a camera operator who makes short, observational films.

His work moves between nature, food, and art, following whatever the camera happens to find along the way. He publishes it on his YouTube channel, B_Borderless.

He is building toward work on film and television sets as a camera operator, and is available for short films, documentary, commercial, and music video productions.
